Live Sensor Signals

Monitoring Your Safety in Real Time

Speed

—

Sudden speed drop can indicate a crash or hard braking event.

Rotation

—

Sharp rotation spikes suggest the device tumbled or spun violently.

Impact

—

High G-force impact is a strong indicator of a collision.

Ye kaam kaise karta hai

  1. Detection ON karte hi phone ke motion aur location sensors chalu ho jaate hain.
  2. Speed, Rotation aur Impact — teeno signal alag-alag monitor hote hain.
  3. Jab teeno ek saath trip ho jaayein, to 20-second countdown shuru hota hai.
  4. Aap theek hain to Cancel dabaakar rok sakte hain.
  5. Cancel na kiya to family/favourites ko location ke saath alert chala jaata hai.
